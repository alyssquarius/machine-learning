# Brand assets

PNG snapshots retrieved 2026-10-28 from [Car Logos Dataset](https://github.com/filippofilip95/car-logos-dataset).
The source URLs for each mark are recorded in sources.json. Brand marks belong to their respective owners and are used to identify vehicle makes; no affiliation or certification is implied.

These files are served locally through Next Image. Their aspect ratios and colors are preserved. A white neutral plate provides contrast in both light and dark themes. Unknown/custom makes use a generic car symbol instead of another brand's logo.

Vehicle body illustrations are custom SVG paths in components/vehicle-icons.tsx, not brand marks.
